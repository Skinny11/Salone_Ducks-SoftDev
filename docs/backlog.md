# Product Backlog - Salone Ducks Document Application System

## User Stories

### US-01: Submit Document Application
**As a** citizen of Sierra Leone,
**I want to** submit an application for a national document (ID Card, Passport, or Driver's License) online,
**so that** I can obtain official identification without visiting a government office in person.

**Use Case Link:** UC-01 (Submit Application)

---

### US-02: Capture and Upload Photo
**As a** citizen applying for a document,
**I want to** either upload a photo from my device or capture one live using my camera,
**so that** I can provide a valid passport-sized photo without needing to visit a photography studio.

**Use Case Link:** UC-02 (Photo Capture)

---

### US-03: Track Application Status
**As a** citizen who has submitted an application,
**I want to** track the status of my application using my Application ID,
**so that** I know when my document is ready for collection.

**Use Case Link:** UC-03 (Track Application)

---

### US-04: Update Application Within 5 Days
**As a** citizen who has submitted an application,
**I want to** update my application details within 5 days of submission,
**so that** I can correct any errors or provide additional information before processing begins.

**Use Case Link:** UC-04 (Update Application)

---

### US-05: Delete Application Within 5 Days
**As a** citizen who has submitted an application,
**I want to** delete my application within 5 days of submission,
**so that** I can cancel the application if I no longer need the document.

**Use Case Link:** UC-05 (Delete Application)

---

### US-06: View Real-Time Application Preview
**As a** citizen filling out an application,
**I want to** see a live preview of my document as I fill in the form,
**so that** I can verify all details are correct before submitting.

**Use Case Link:** UC-06 (Application Preview)

---

### US-07: Age Validation for Driver's License
**As a** citizen applying for a Driver's License,
**I want to** be automatically informed if I am below the minimum age of 18,
**so that** I don't waste time completing an application that will be rejected.

**Use Case Link:** UC-07 (Age Validation)

---

### US-08: Subscribe to News and Updates
**As a** citizen,
**I want to** subscribe to receive news, holiday announcements, and job vacancies via email,
**so that** I stay informed about government services and opportunities.

**Use Case Link:** UC-08 (Subscribe)

---

### US-09: Filter News by Category
**As a** citizen browsing the news section,
**I want to** filter news articles by category (Press Releases, Holidays, Job Vacancies),
**so that** I can quickly find information relevant to my interests.

**Use Case Link:** UC-09 (Filter News)

---

### US-10: Get Directions to Service Center
**As a** citizen who needs to collect a document,
**I want to** get directions to the nearest service center,
**so that** I can easily find the location and plan my visit.

**Use Case Link:** UC-10 (Get Directions)

---

### US-11: View Service Center Information
**As a** citizen,
**I want to** view contact details and addresses of all service centers,
**so that** I know where to go for document collection or inquiries.

**Use Case Link:** UC-11 (Service Center Info)


### US-12: Contact Support
**As a** citizen experiencing issues with my application,
**I want to** send a message to the support team,
**so that** I can get help with my application or report problems.

**Use Case Link:** UC-12 (Contact Support)

---

### US-13: View Weather and Time
**As a** citizen visiting the Salone Ducks website,
**I want to** view the current time and weather information,
**so that** I can plan my visit to a service center accordingly.

**Use Case Link:** UC-13 (Weather and Time)

---

### US-14: Receive Email Confirmation
**As a** citizen who has submitted an application,
**I want to** receive an email confirmation with my Application ID and payment details,
**so that** I have a record of my application and know how to pay the required fee.

**Use Case Link:** UC-14 (Email Confirmation)

---
