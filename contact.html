Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my application?

Use your Application ID on the Apply page to track, update, or delete your application.

What are the processing times?

National ID: 7–14 days | Passport: 14–21 days | Driver's License: 7–14 days.

How do I pay for my documents?

Pay at any Orange Money or Afrimoney agent using the payment reference generated with your Application ID.

Can I update my application after submission?

Yes, within 5 days of submission. Use your Application ID on the Apply page.
